Peer Assessment — You have a $1,000 bonus to distribute among your teammates (not including yourself). Drag and drop bills onto each teammate's card to allocate funds. You may give any amount — or nothing — to each person, as long as the total equals exactly $1,000. You must also leave a brief written comment for each teammate.

Guidelines for allocation:

  • Base your allocation on each teammate's overall contribution to the team's success.
  • Consider factors such as preparation, reliability, effort, quality of work, and participation.
  • An equal split (around $333 each for a 3-teammate team) is appropriate when all teammates contributed equally.
  • Use the "Split Evenly" button as a starting point, then adjust as needed.

Comments — Be specific and constructive. Your comments will be shared with your instructor only and will inform individual feedback.

Your responses are confidential. Individual allocations and comments will not be shared with your teammates.
